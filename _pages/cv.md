Hi! I was born and raised in İstanbul, where I went to Galatasaray High School. I started university in chemical engineering at Boğaziçi University, then switched to the social sciences and graduated in 2021 with a B.A. in Political Science and International Relations and a double major in Economics. I went on to earn an M.A. in Political Science at Sabancı University in 2023.

I am now pursuing a Ph.D. in Government at The University of Texas at Austin, where I work with Prof. Zeynep Somer-Topcu, and an M.S. in Statistics alongside it. I study party politics, electoral behavior and election campaigns in Europe. My dissertation asks how different understandings of legitimacy shape voters' evaluations of parties through negative campaigning.

Along the way, I worked as a junior data scientist building deep learning models to detect breast cancer, and helped grow a hackerspace in İstanbul for people interested in algorithms and artificial intelligence.
