I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Government at The University of Texas at Austin, where I am also pursuing an M.S. in Statistics. My research focuses on party politics, electoral behavior, and election campaigns, with an empirical focus on European politics.

Methodologically, I work primarily with survey experiments, causal inference, and text analysis to study how voters evaluate parties and respond to campaigns.

Feel free to look through my [research](/research/), [teaching](/teaching/) and [CV](/cv/) for more detail, or [reach out](/contact/) if you'd like to be in touch.
